## Included Media

- Existing driver card crops, team car cutouts, the F1 mark, and other artwork were supplied or approved for the original fan project. Their presence is not proof of an open redistribution license.
- The higher-resolution Rafael Camara, Ryo Hirakawa, and Yuki Tsunoda portraits came from the official Formula 1 pages listed in [the asset source notes](../public/f1-assets/README.md).

- The original supplemental uploads remain as unused source references; the app uses the higher-resolution `*-portrait.webp` files.
